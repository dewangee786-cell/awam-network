Platform Governance

Privacy Policy

The platform is intended to collect and process only information necessary for account operation, learning, assessment, certification and approved platform services. Specific data handling, retention and deletion rules should be finalized before public production launch.

Terms & Conditions

Users are expected to use the platform lawfully, honestly and responsibly. Abuse, fraud, impersonation, cheating, unauthorized access and misuse of platform services may result in account restrictions according to the finalized policy.

AI Usage Policy

AI is intended to assist learning, generate educational material, provide explanations, analyze progress and support practical learning. Sensitive administrative actions require appropriate permission and human oversight.

Exam Policy

Examinations may include timed tests, practical assessments and final evaluations. Exam integrity, identity controls, monitoring and retesting rules will be implemented as the examination backend is developed.

Certificate Policy

Completion of lessons alone does not automatically guarantee certification. Eligibility can depend on the approved combination of learning, homework, practical work, tests, final examination and project requirements. Final approval remains under authorized Super Admin control.

Account & Security Policy

Accounts may be restricted, suspended or reviewed where platform rules, security requirements or abuse-prevention procedures require action.
