Security Principles

Production architecture
  • Firebase Authentication required before Hub access.
  • Role changes are protected by Firebase Rules.
  • Wallet balance is not controlled by normal users.
  • Exam scores are not trusted from the browser.
  • AI answers are backend-controlled.
  • Verification statuses are administration-controlled.
  • Audit logs are protected from ordinary users.
